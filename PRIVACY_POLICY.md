# Privacy Policy for Charlotte Discord Bot

*Last Updated: November 12, 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how **Charlotte** ("the Bot", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with the Bot across Discord servers.

We value your privacy and only process the minimal data necessary to provide voice channel moderation features.

---

## 1. Information We Process
To provide voice channel management commands, Charlotte temporarily processes:
- **Discord User IDs & Channel IDs:** To identify which voice channel an administrator is in and which users are connected to it.
- **Server Permissions:** To verify whether the user executing commands has administrative or "Mute Members" authority.
- **Voice State Information:** To determine if members in a channel are currently muted or unmuted.

Charlotte **DOES NOT**:
- Record, listen to, or store any voice audio.
- Store private message logs or chat conversations.
- Collect personal identifying information such as emails, passwords, or payment details.
- Sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.

---

## 2. Data Storage & Retention
Charlotte operates in real-time. Transient data (such as temporary voice channel rosters) is kept only in volatile memory during command execution and is not written to permanent databases.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services
Charlotte connects to Discord via the official [Discord API](https://discord.com/developers/docs/intro). Your interactions are also governed by [Discord's Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Your Rights
Under applicable data privacy regulations, you have rights regarding your personal data. Because Charlotte does not store personal records or persistent user databases, no persistent personal data is held. 

If you wish to remove Charlotte from your server, any server administrator can kick or ban the bot at any time.

---

## 5. Security
We take reasonable security measures to protect the Bot and its operational environment. Bot tokens and sensitive credentials are held in secure environment configurations and are never publicly exposed.

---

## 6. Updates to This Policy
We may revise this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted to this document with an updated revision date.

---

## 7. Contact
For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the official GitHub repository.
